## Prism-Guides

"Prism-Guides" provide an overview of Prism's functionality, guide through specific workflows, and offer tips and tricks to get the most out of the pipeline.

Guides are organized into sections like Setup, Project Browser, or Houdini. Each section includes categories such as Basics, Extras, or USD for easy navigation and focused learning.

Hint

Click any category thumbnail to access the corresponding guides.
